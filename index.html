ABOUT POLARIS

Polaris is a company based in The Netherlands that provides specialized software for two important industries: Earth Observation and UAV/Drone technology and our capabilities provide you with the necessary support to develop high quality engineering solutions for your business.

Our services and our capacities provide you with the necessary support to organize complex data and GIS/IoT data with your objectives into more sophisticated data tools and formats, making it easily understandable with the aid of algorithms. We have developed products for academia and research institutions as well as for large corporations in their regional markets, as we deliver your projects in compliance with technical requirements, technical knowledge, commercial expertise and with the 360 of our experts.
